# Enablement — Incident Precedent Nudge (5–10 min)

## What it is

An **AI-generated advisory** tip when you review an investigation task. It may show:

- A **similar past incident** (with optional one-line root cause / action summary) and a link to open it
- A **warning** that similar reports at this location were closed as not valid / duplicate
- Or a short note that nothing similar was found

## What it is not

- Not a verified fact  
- **Does not** fill Major Root Cause, category, or closure fields  
- **Does not** block Claim, Forward, Open Task, or completing your investigation  

OSHA / investigation quality still require **your** investigation.

## How to use

1. Open your task in **My Inbox** (Root Causes Hierarchy or Review and complete investigation).
2. Read the suggestion under the assignment text (teaser).
3. Optionally open **Manage Incidents → Investigation** for the full panel.
4. Click **Open in Manage Incidents** to view the historical record.
5. If the suggestion is wrong, click **Not relevant** — that helps us improve later (logged only in v1).

## Tips

- Treat summaries as **hints**, then verify in the full incident.
- For false/dup warnings: verify details before investing full investigation effort — you can still proceed.
